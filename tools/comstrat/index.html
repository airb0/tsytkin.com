About the brand: me

I'm a creative director. I spent fifteen years in international advertising agencies, mostly on the receiving end of the creative brief.

Brand Value Proposition

I believe an idea is only as strong as the truth about people it rests on. That's why I look for the tension in people's lives and make it clear to everyone who works on the job. I do it with ideas, campaigns and activations — and with the briefs and platforms behind them.

About the product

The news

I created comstrat, a Claude Code skill for communication strategy. It is now available publicly under the MIT licence.

Product CVP

Every creative team deserves a brief it can start thinking from. That's why comstrat turns a pile of client inputs into a readable task: a Get-To-By brief with a real insight, a brand platform and a message system. It does it with seven rule files — the method I would want behind every brief I receive.

Product CVP: Why, How, What
Why
comstrat exists so that every creative team starts from a truth about people, not from a pile of inputs.
How
It takes the creative pair's side and makes an unclear task readable.
What
It builds the Get-To-By brief, the insight, the CVP on Why-How-What, the message ladder, the archetype pair, principles and tone — and asks before it invents.
Product CVP by audience

For strategists, comstrat brings confidence that the platform holds: the ladder checks the circle, and a break report names the failing step, so the platform survives the creative stage.

Creative pairs get a task they can start from on day one — a Get-To-By brief with an “I want X, but Y” insight and a list of open questions — so their hours go into ideas, not into decoding.

Solo creators and small studios get a strategist's discipline without a strategy department: the full platform in presentation order, ready for a deck, so small clients get the same quality of thinking.

Message ladder
  1. 1What we docomstrat reads the client's inputs, asks the missing questions and writes briefs, propositions and ladders by fixed rules.
  2. 2What we createso that there is one task and one brand meaning everyone on the job reads the same way.
  3. 3For the personso that the creative pair knows what to make before they start.
  4. 4For the person, sharperso that the team spends its hours on ideas, not on decoding the brief.
  5. 5For the systemso that the work that reaches people rests on a real truth about them.
  6. 6For peopleso that people get communication that means something to them.
Principles
  • Ask before inventingWhen the inputs are thin, list the questions and wait; don't fill holes with plausible guesses.
  • Every sentence carries meaningUse the client's own words wherever they fit, but no sentence is there just to fill space.
  • One audience, one shiftA brief that wants everything gets nothing.
  • Test what you writeEvery tool ends with its checks.
Tone of voice
  • ShortSay it in as few words as it takes.No tension, no insight.An insight should ideally contain some form of tension between a desire and a barrier.
  • PlainThe words the reader already uses, including the client's own; no jargon to sound expert.
  • To the pointEvery line tells the reader something they can use.

Here's how a brief for communicating this skill could look

Brief: Get-To-By
Get
creative teams and strategists who receive briefsI want to start making the work, but what I got is a pile of inputs that doesn't say what the task is.
To
move from “the brief is whatever the client sends” to “I can turn any inputs into a brief I can start from.”
By
a Claude Code skill that reads the inputs, asks what is missing and returns a Get-To-By brief and a platform, with the gaps listed.
Key messages
  1. Drop in the client's inputs, get a task you can start from.
  2. No tension, no insight.
  3. The ladder checks the circle.
  4. It asks before it invents.
How to install and use

Copy the folder into your project's .claude/skills/comstrat/, or into ~/.claude/skills/comstrat/ to use it in every project.

git clone https://github.com/airb0/comstrat.git ~/.claude/skills/comstrat

Then ask Claude Code in your own words:

  • make a brief from these inputs
  • Get-To-By
  • write the value proposition
  • build the message ladder
  • pick the brand archetype
  • check our platform for holes
