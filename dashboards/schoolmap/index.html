How to read the map. Click a blue pin (or “Show on map”) to see that school’s catchment area (secteur scolaire) for 2026–2027. Public Paris schools admit by home address, not by distance. International sections admit by application and language assessment, and private schools by their own process, so they have no catchment area. Suburban catchment areas are not shown.

Method. Included: schools where both CP and an English programme are confirmed (a bilingual programme or a British/American international section). Ordinary English lessons (LV1) do not count as a bilingual programme. Check places, campus and admission rules for 2026–2027 before applying.

Indicators. IPS (indice de position sociale) is the ministry’s index of pupils’ family social background, 2024–2025: national average about 106; higher means more advantaged families. It is not a measure of teaching quality. Pupils and classes are for the 2025 school year. REP / REP+ means the school is in the priority education network. Source: data.education.gouv.fr (IPS des écoles; Effectifs d’élèves par école).

Locations. School coordinates and addresses come from the official Annuaire de l’éducation (data.education.gouv.fr, November 2026), which also corrected several addresses. Apartment addresses are looked up with the Base Adresse Nationale (api-adresse.data.gouv.fr). Catchment areas: DASCO / Ville de Paris.
