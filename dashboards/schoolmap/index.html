Method. Included: schools where both CP and an English programme are confirmed (a bilingual programme or a British/American international section). Ordinary English lessons (LV1) do not count as a bilingual programme. The suburbs (92, 93, 94 and the part of 78 near Paris) were searched in November 2026 for public bilingual programmes and private bilingual schools under contract: none was found in 92, 93 or 94 beyond the international sections; private schools without a state contract (hors contrat) are not included. Indicators. IPS and REP: data.education.gouv.fr (IPS des écoles, 2024–2025). Pupils and classes: Effectifs d’élèves par école, 2025.

Locations. School coordinates and addresses come from the official Annuaire de l’éducation (data.education.gouv.fr, November 2026). Apartment addresses are looked up with the Base Adresse Nationale (api-adresse.data.gouv.fr). Catchment areas: DASCO / Ville de Paris.
